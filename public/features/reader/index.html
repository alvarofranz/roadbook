The navigator: follow a roadbook turn by turn with GPS. A paper-style note table, a live odometer and CAP direction bar, automatic or manual validation, and full competition scoring with a signed result.

Open the Reader

Paper-style note table

Each note shows distance, the tulip vignette and your comments, colour-coded by state — reached, skipped, active, approaching.

Odometer & CAP bar

Total and partial distance, speed and heading up top; a CAP direction bar points you along the prescribed heading.

Automatic or manual

GPS validates each note within its detection radius (per-note, the roadbook default, or a fixed radius), or tap "reached" yourself.

Per-note map

An optional MapLibre mini-map per note, satellite or topo, to confirm the position on the ground.

Competition mode

Validates with penalties and emits an HMAC-signed result QR for the Ranking — tamper-evident scoring.

Load & record

Open your own, a public roadbook, or a .rdbk from the OS; crash-safe GPX logging, sound on note, and background GPS in the native app.

Tips & tricks

Pick the mode at start

The start screen sets Trip vs Competition and automatic (GPS) vs manual advancement — choose before you roll.

Tune the detection radius

Automatic mode marks a note when you're within its detection radius; the start modal can pin one fixed radius for the whole run if the per-note values don't suit the terrain.

Keep the odometer honest

Nudge the distance ±10 m when needed; validating a note syncs the total to that note's distance, so a wrong reading self-corrects at each waypoint.

Use the per-note map

Open a note's mini-map (satellite or topo) to confirm the spot on the ground when the text alone is ambiguous.

Competition → keep the QR

Competition mode scores with penalties and emits an HMAC-signed result QR — hand it to the Ranking; it can't be forged.

Log the run, screen locked

Optional crash-safe GPX logging plus a sound on each note; in the native app GPS keeps running with the screen off.
