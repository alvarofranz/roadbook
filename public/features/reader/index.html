Tripmaster

The navigator: follow a roadbook turn by turn with GPS. A paper-style note table, a live odometer and CAP direction bar, automatic or manual validation, and full competition scoring with a signed result.

Open the Reader

Paper-style note table

Each note shows distance, the tulip vignette and your comments, colour-coded by state — reached, skipped, active, approaching.

Odometer & CAP bar

Total and partial distance, speed and heading up top; a CAP direction bar points you along the prescribed heading.

Automatic or manual

GPS validates each note within its detection radius (per-note, the roadbook default, or a fixed radius), or tap "reached" yourself.

Per-note map

An optional MapLibre mini-map per note, satellite or topo, to confirm the position on the ground.

Competition mode

Validates with penalties and emits an HMAC-signed result QR for the Ranking — tamper-evident scoring.

Load & record

Open your own, a public roadbook, or a .rdbk from the OS; crash-safe GPX logging, sound on note, and background GPS in the native app.

Works onWindowsmacOS / iOSAndroidLinux — free, installable PWA + native apps.
