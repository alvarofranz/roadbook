Forest trails and gravel roads often look alike on a map. A roadbook adds the small details: the fork beside a gate, a landmark before a climb or the distance to the next junction. Prepare a mountain-bike loop, a gravel day out or an orientation ride for your club, with notes that help riders recognise the route on the ground.

Before you set off

How can I share a route with my cycling club?

Save the roadbook to your account and publish it in the public gallery, or share its exported .rdbk file with the group. Riders can open it in RDBK on their own devices. For an organised outing, event tools bring participants and roadbooks together.

Can I follow a roadbook without mobile data?

Open your roadbook before setting off. A .rdbk file contains its track, notes and symbols, and GPS positioning does not require mobile data. Online maps, downloading shared roadbooks and syncing your account still need a connection.

Browser or native app: which should I use?

Use the web Editor on a large screen to prepare your route. On the trail, the iOS and Android apps add background GPS, so a recording can continue with the screen locked. Use the same .rdbk roadbook across devices.
