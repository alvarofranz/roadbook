Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 June 2026

RDBK.app ("RDBK", "we") is a free suite for creating and navigating digital roadbooks, available as a website, an installable web app (PWA) and native apps for Android and iOS. This policy explains what data RDBK handles and why. The short version: your location is used on your device to navigate and record routes; an account and cloud storage are entirely optional; we show no ads, run no third-party tracking and never sell your data.

Location

  • When you use the Reader, Tripmaster or Recorder, RDBK reads your GPS location to show your position, compute the odometer/heading and follow the roadbook.
  • If you record a route, your track is logged as a GPX file on your device. In the native app, recording can continue with the screen locked via a foreground service (shown as an ongoing notification) so the track has no gaps.
  • Your live location is not sent to our servers. A recorded route only leaves your device if you choose to save it to your account.

Account (optional)

  • The core tools work without an account. If you create one, we store your first/last name, username and email, and a securely hashed password.
  • An account lets you save roadbooks to your profile, attach photos and publish public challenges.

Roadbooks & photos (only if you save them)

  • When you save a roadbook to your profile, its data and any geotagged photos you add are stored on our server so you can access them across devices.
  • Each roadbook is private by default; it becomes visible to others only if you publish it as a public challenge.

Camera & photos

The camera is used only when you choose to add a photo to a roadbook. Photos you attach are uploaded to your account; photos are never collected automatically.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising and no third-party advertising/analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No background collection of your location outside an active recording you started.

Storage & security

Data you save is stored on our server in the EU. Passwords and access tokens are stored hashed. Connections use HTTPS.

Your choices & rights

  • Use RDBK fully without an account (no personal data leaves your device).
  • Edit or delete your roadbooks and photos at any time from your account.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us.

Children

RDBK is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.

Contact

Questions or data requests: info@rdbk.app.
